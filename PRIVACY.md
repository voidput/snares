# Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. The same text is shown on the site, under Privacy in the footer (`src/components/Policies.tsx`). Keep the two in step.

Short version: Snares has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. Your plans never leave your browser unless you share them yourself.

## What stays in your browser

- **Saved plans.** Plans you save, and whether the result panel is open, are kept in your browser's local storage. They are not sent anywhere. Delete a plan in the Plans tab, or clear this site's data in your browser, and they are gone.
- **The plan on screen.** It is written into the page address after the `#`. Browsers do not send that part to any server. Anyone you give the link to can see the plan in it.
- **Exports and imports.** An exported file is made on your device. An imported file is read on your device and is not uploaded.
- **Pasted positions.** A `/showlocation` line you paste is used on the page and is not stored or sent.
- **Clipboard.** The site writes to your clipboard only when you press a copy button. It never reads it.

## What the host sees

The site is static files served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when the pages load, and may log them under its own privacy statement. The site's author has no access to those logs. Fonts and game data are served from this same site; the page contacts no other server.

## Links that leave the site

Coffee opens Buy Me a Coffee and Enlist opens robertsspaceindustries.com, each in a new tab. Nothing is sent to them until you click, and what happens there falls under their policies.

## Children, sale of data, your rights

The site collects no personal data, so there is none to sell, share, correct or delete on a server, and nothing is collected from children. Everything the site keeps is in your own browser, under your control.

## Changes

If the site starts to handle data differently, this page changes first and the date above with it.
